Support

Stuck, or found a ticket Stash reads wrong? Email stashtickets@gmail.com — a screenshot helps.

Adding a ticket

  • From an email: open the PDF or ticket, tap Share, then Stash.
  • Screenshot: tap + → Add ticket → Photos and pick it.
  • PDF: tap + → Add ticket → PDF from Files.
  • Paper ticket: tap + → Add ticket → Scan a paper ticket.
  • Booking website: in Safari, tap Share → Stash, sign in if needed, then Capture ticket.

Adding a stay

Tap + → Add stay, then pick screenshots or a PDF of your booking from Airbnb, Booking.com or your host's messages. Stash reads the place, dates, address, how you get in, house code, Wi-Fi, arrival directions and contact number. Tap the pencil next to anything to change it, then Save stay. To change a saved stay, open it and tap ⋯ → Edit details.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Stash uses it to read tricky tickets and bookings. It runs on your iPhone; nothing is sent anywhere. Turn it on or off in Stash's Settings → Reading tickets. If the switch can't be turned on, switch on Apple Intelligence in the iPhone's Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri.

Reminders aren't showing

Open Settings in Stash and tap Turn on reminders. Then in the iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Stash, allow notifications and set Show Previews to Always.

The widget

Press and hold your home screen, tap +, search for Stash and add the Next ticket widget.

Your data

Everything stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
