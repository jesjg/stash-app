Adding a ticket

  • From an email: open the PDF or ticket, tap Share, then Stash.
  • Screenshot: tap + → Photos and pick it.
  • PDF: tap + → PDF from Files.
  • Paper ticket: tap + → Scan a paper ticket.
  • Booking link: tap + → Ticket link, sign in if needed, then Capture ticket.

Check-in details for a stay

Tap + → Paste check-in details and paste the host's message, or add screenshots of the check-in screens with + → Photos. Stash picks out the address, times, door code and Wi-Fi.

Reminders aren't showing

Open Settings in Stash and tap Turn on reminders. Then in the iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Stash, allow notifications and set Show Previews to Always.

The widget

Press and hold your home screen, tap +, search for Stash and add the Next ticket widget.

Your data

Everything stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
